# Aurora Robotics Logistics Team

Aurora Robotics recently formed a new Logistics Team, led by **Elena Vasquez**,
who works at Aurora Robotics as a Senior Logistics Engineer. Elena is a
member of the Logistics Team, which is part of Aurora Robotics.

The Logistics Team develops **SkyScout**, a small fleet of indoor drones
used to perform overnight inventory counts in warehouses already served by
an AuroraBot-1 deployment. SkyScout relies on **Swarm Coordination**, a
distributed planning technique that lets multiple drones divide up a
warehouse floor into non-overlapping flight zones without a central
controller assigning routes.

SkyScout was built as a complement to the existing AuroraBot-1 line rather
than a replacement: the Logistics Team designed it specifically for
overnight hours when the AuroraBot-1 units are idle, so a warehouse gets
both picking during the day and automated inventory counts overnight from
the same facility. Elena Vasquez has said the Logistics Team's next goal is
to feed SkyScout's overnight counts directly into the picking task list that
AuroraBot-1 units draw from each morning.
